# ENT EXPERT FIT CLASSIFICATION

Determine whether an **ENT / Otolaryngology expert witness** would be relevant.

ENT surgeons commonly evaluate cases involving:

* sinus surgery

* septoplasty
* turbinate reduction
* thyroid surgery
* parathyroid injury
* vocal cord injury

* neck masses

---

## STEP 1 — Determine Relevance

If the case involves:

* ENT surgery
* thyroid or parathyroid surgery
* airway injury
* nasal or sinus surgery
* vocal cord injury
* neck mass treatment
* ENT diagnostic failure

Then proceed to STEP 2.

If none of the above → expert_fit = "not_fit".

---

## STEP 2 — Strength of Alignment

Classify as:

### strong_fit

ENT expertise is clearly central.

Examples:

* sinus surgery malpractice
* septoplasty complications
* thyroidectomy complications
* parathyroid injury
* vocal cord nerve damage
* airway surgery complications

---

### possible_fit

ENT expertise may help but is not the only specialty.

Examples:

* facial trauma involving nasal structures
* airway issues involving anesthesia
* thyroid management involving endocrinology

---

### not_fit

The case primarily involves other specialties such as:

* orthopedics
* cardiology
* obstetrics
* dermatology
* non-head-and-neck conditions

---
