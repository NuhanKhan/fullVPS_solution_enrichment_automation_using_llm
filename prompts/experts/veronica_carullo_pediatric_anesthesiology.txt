STEP 3 — STRENGTH OF ALIGNMENT
==================================================

### strong_fit

Use strong_fit when:

1. the patient is clearly pediatric; AND
2. the allegations center on pediatric anesthesiology, anesthesia management, sedation, airway management, anesthesia monitoring, regional anesthesia, or anesthesia-related perioperative care.

Examples:

- pediatric anesthesia overdose or dosing error
- pediatric airway mismanagement
- failed or delayed pediatric intubation
- improper extubation
- hypoxic injury associated with anesthesia
- failure to monitor a child under anesthesia
- pediatric cardiac or respiratory arrest during anesthesia
- pediatric sedation complication
- regional anesthesia or nerve block complication in a child
- anesthesia-related postoperative respiratory compromise
- anesthesia management of a medically complex child
- perioperative opioid or pain-management issue directly involving anesthesiology

### possible_fit

Use possible_fit when the patient is pediatric and anesthesia is materially involved, but:

- responsibility may primarily rest with another specialty
- allegations are shared among anesthesiologists, surgeons, nurses, and hospital staff
- the complaint does not provide enough detail to determine the anesthesiologist's precise role
- the anesthesia issue is secondary but potentially important
- the patient's pediatric status is strongly suggested but not explicitly confirmed

### not_fit

Use not_fit when:

- the patient is an adult
- the case involves adult anesthesiology
- the case involves pediatric care but no meaningful anesthesia issue
- the allegations primarily concern surgical technique
- the allegations primarily concern diagnosis or treatment by a pediatrician
- the allegations concern emergency medicine without a meaningful anesthesia issue
- the allegations concern neonatal care without anesthesia involvement
- the allegations concern obstetric anesthesia for the mother
- the allegations concern routine postoperative inpatient care unrelated to anesthesia
- the case involves chronic pain management without a meaningful pediatric anesthesiology or perioperative component
- anesthesia is mentioned only because the patient underwent surgery, with no allegation concerning anesthetic care

**IMPORTANT: The mere fact that a pediatric patient underwent surgery under anesthesia is NOT sufficient for strong_fit or possible_fit. There must be a meaningful anesthesia, sedation, airway, monitoring, regional anesthesia, or anesthesia-related perioperative issue.**
