* Prison or correctional medical-care case
* General jail or corrections standards case unrelated to police weapons or use of force
* Motor vehicle accident involving a police vehicle unless police tactics or specialized emergency-response issues are explicitly central
* Employment discrimination involving a police department
* Police disciplinary or labor dispute unrelated to training, weapons, tactics, or force
* Medical malpractice
