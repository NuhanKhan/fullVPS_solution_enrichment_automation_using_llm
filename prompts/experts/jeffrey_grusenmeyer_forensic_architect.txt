CLASSIFICATION RULES:

construction_defect:
The central allegations involve defective workmanship, improper installation, deficient materials, deviation from plans or specifications, or failure of completed construction.

design_error_or_omission:
The central allegations involve defective architectural plans, incomplete drawings, improper specifications, coordination failures, unsuitable material selection, or another alleged design deficiency.

building_code_or_permitting:

plan_review_or_building_inspection:

building_envelope_or_fenestration:

roofing_or_waterproofing:

construction_administration_or_project_management:

premises_liability_built_environment:
The case involves bodily injury allegedly caused by an architectural or physical property condition such as stairs, ramps, handrails, guardrails, walking surfaces, doors, exits, lighting, elevation changes, or circulation design.

ada_or_accessibility:
The central issue involves ADA compliance, accessibility standards, accessible routes, ramps, entrances, parking, restrooms, or accommodations for individuals with disabilities.

property_damage_building_failure:
The case involves significant damage or failure of a building or building component, but the complaint does not clearly identify a narrower category.

construction_contract_or_payment_dispute:
The case primarily concerns payment, liens, scope, delay, change orders, contractual obligations, or project accounting rather than a technical defect or architectural standard-of-care issue.

other_architecture_or_construction_case:
Architecture, construction, or building conditions are central, but no more specific category applies.

non_architecture_or_construction_case:
Architecture, construction defects, building codes, design, or physical building conditions are not central to the dispute.
